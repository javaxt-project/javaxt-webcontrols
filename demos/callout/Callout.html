Callout Demo

A callout is a popup box with an arrow. showAt(x, y, position, align) places the tip of the arrow at a point: position is the side of the point the box appears on, and align is how the box lines up with the arrow. The red crosshairs mark the points passed to showAt(), and the line under each demo compares them with where the arrow tip ended up. Clicking outside a callout hides it.

Positions and alignments

Every combination of position and align. Extra height is added to some of the left/right callouts to demonstrate top/bottom alignment.

Sectors

Move the cursor around the box. The space around it is divided into 12 sectors, 3 along each side, and the callout is shown on the side of the box the cursor is on, extending towards the cursor.
