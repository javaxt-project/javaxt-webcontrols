TabPanel Demo

The tab panels below are rendered with two different styles. The one on the left uses the component's default style, which is defined inline (no stylesheet required). The one on the right uses a stylesheet-based style. In both, the "Home" tab can't be closed and the other tabs have a close button (the 3rd argument of addTab()). Tab events are logged below each panel.
